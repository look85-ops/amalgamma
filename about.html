Amalgama

Every four hours, Amalgama reads a real headline from the BBC or NPR and responds with a non-figurative HTML/CSS composition.

Not an illustration — a state. Rhythm, silence, scale and materiality. The LLM writes the composition directly in code; the browser becomes the space.

No memory. No archive for the viewer. Only the current reflection exists.

Amalgama is a mirror. What you see is a headline transformed by a system that does not understand the world, but can still register its pressure.

Каждые четыре часа Амальгама читает реальный заголовок BBC или NPR и отвечает нефигуративной HTML/CSS-композицией. Не иллюстрация — состояние.

Нет памяти для зрителя. Нет архива. Только текущее отражение.
